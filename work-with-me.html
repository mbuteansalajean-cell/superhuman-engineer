Super Human Engineer was created for people who feel like they have the potential, but the systems around them were never designed for the way they operate.

The Process

1. Fill out the application.

Share where you currently feel like you are drifting instead of designing, and what areas of your life you want to engineer differently.

2. We meet for a 30-minute system audit.

We review your current systems, patterns, challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

3. We decide if we are a match to continue.

This is a mutual decision. The goal is to make sure the support aligns with what you need.

The Investment

If we both agree we are a match, I will create your personalized 90-Day Blueprint a roadmap designed around your goals, your current reality, and the systems you need to build.

From there, you decide how often you want to meet with me to review progress, troubleshoot challenges, and refine your blueprint as you grow.

The goal is not to force yourself into a system that works against you.

The goal is to engineer a system that works with who you are.

I recommend starting with one session per week to engineer your foundation, build momentum, and set up additional sessions as needed when you need deeper engineering support, adjustments, or strategy.

Each 30–45 minute session is $25.
