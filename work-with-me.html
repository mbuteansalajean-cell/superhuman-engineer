Work With Me

Build a life that works with your brain, not against it.

Super Human Engineer was created for people who feel like they have the potential, but the systems around them were never designed for the way they operate.

This is not about fixing who you are. It is about engineering systems that work with who you are.

The Process

1. Fill out the application.

Share where you currently feel like you are drifting instead of designing, and what areas of your life you want to engineer differently.

2. We meet for a 30 minute system audit.

We review your current systems, patterns, challenges, and opportunities for improvement.

3. We decide if we are a match to continue.

This is a mutual decision. The goal is to make sure the support aligns with what you need.

4. We review your 90 Day Blueprint in your first session.

If we agree we are a match, you will book your first 60 minute session for $99. I will create your personalized 90 Day Blueprint and walk you through it during that session so you leave with a clear, actionable path forward.

The Investment

If we both agree we are a match, your first 60 minute session is $99. This first session is required so we can review your personalized 90 Day Blueprint together and make sure you understand how to put it into action.

After that first session, additional sessions are completely optional. You can book as many or as few as you would like to review progress, troubleshoot challenges, and refine your blueprint as you grow.

For ongoing momentum, accountability, and deeper engineering support, I recommend meeting biweekly or monthly.
