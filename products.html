Questions

Before you begin

Is this a live course?

No. Super Human Engineer is a self paced digital workbook system. Each level is designed as a 12 week, 90 day journey, but you can move through it at the pace that supports you.

How do I use the digital workbook?

You can print the workbook and KPI Tracker to write in them by hand. You do not have to print everything at once. You can print each weekly section as you reach it. If you prefer working digitally, you can import the PDFs into an annotation app such as Goodnotes, Notability, Adobe Acrobat, or Apple Preview to write, highlight, and add notes.

Do I need to be neurodivergent?

No diagnosis is required. The material is created with neurodivergent experiences in mind and is for anyone who wants to understand their patterns and build more supportive personal systems.

Where should I start?

Begin with Level 1: Buddha if you are new to the system. Level 2 and Level 3 build on that foundation. If you are ready to commit to the full journey, the bundle includes all three at a 20% savings.

What comes with my purchase?

Every purchase includes the selected digital workbook, the Super Human Engineer Orientation Guide, and the 30 Day KPI Tracker. The complete bundle includes all three workbooks plus both companion resources.

Is this therapy or medical treatment?

No. These materials are for education and personal development and are not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, psychiatric, or therapeutic advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
