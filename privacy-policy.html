Privacy & Analytics

This page explains what the Super Human Engineer website measures, why we measure it, and how you can control your choice.

Optional website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 only after you select “Accept analytics.” It helps us understand visits, traffic sources, pages viewed, purchases, bookings, and interactions with important website buttons. If you decline, the Google Analytics tag is not loaded and analytics cookies are not set by this website.

Information that may be measured

Analytics may process information such as the page visited, approximate location, device and browser type, referral source, campaign tags, time of visit, scroll activity, outbound link clicks, checkout starts, and purchase events. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, workbook responses, Calendly answers, or payment details to Google Analytics.

How we configure Google Analytics

Advertising storage, advertising user data, ad personalization, Google signals, and personalized-ad features are disabled in our website configuration. Google Analytics uses its own systems to process the measurement data. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Stripe and Calendly

Purchases are processed by Stripe and appointments are scheduled through Calendly. When you follow those links, their privacy policies and data practices apply. This website records the relevant button click only if you accepted analytics. Stripe redirects completed purchases back to this website so a purchase conversion can be recorded without sending your payment information to Google Analytics.

Your choice

Your analytics preference is stored in your browser so the website can remember it. You can reopen the choice at any time. Declining or changing your preference does not affect your ability to browse, purchase, or book.

Questions

If you have a privacy question, contact Super Human Engineer using the same email address shown on your purchase receipt or delivery email.
