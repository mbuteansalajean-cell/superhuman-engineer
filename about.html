About Maria Butean-Salajean

I built this because I could not afford anything else.

I grew up in an immigrant family with no fortune to pass down. There were no specialized tutors. No psychiatrist. No therapy. Not because anyone believed those things were unnecessary, but because they were never within reach.

I did eventually get access to some of that support. I was around thirty. By then I had already spent nearly a decade doing the only thing that had ever been available to me, which was figuring it out myself.

The experiments

I was twenty one when I started. I had begun to notice that there were patterns in my life I could not leave behind no matter what changed around me. New city, new job, new plan, same patterns.

So I read everything I could get my hands on. I journaled. I ran experiments on myself in thirty day cycles, some stretching to a full year, and I documented every one of them.

This was never a side interest. It was the only path I could see to the life I wanted, and I did not have a backup.

I made myself one promise: if I ever found my way through, I would keep a record precise enough to hand to somebody else.

What the work kept pointing to

Lasting change did not come from trying harder or thinking differently.

It came from engineering the environment. My day. My workspace. How I learned. Who I spent time around. The conditions I had been treating as fixed turned out, almost all of them, to be designable. Change the conditions and the behavior follows, which is the reverse of how most advice tells you to do it.

That was the only method that produced change I could keep.

Why Super Human Engineer

Engineer is the verb, and it carries the whole idea.

Super Human is not a claim that you will become more than human. It describes what becomes available when the system around you finally fits how you are actually built, instead of how you were told you should be.

Plenty of good frameworks already exist. I read them. A few tactics landed and the rest quietly did not, which makes sense, because we are different enough from one another that no single script fits everyone. When it does not work, you assume the problem is you.

This is not a script. It is a method for building your own.

Why it costs what it costs

The people most likely to need this are often the people least able to spend hundreds of dollars finding out whether it works.

I was one of those people for a long time. So the price reflects that on purpose. What took me years and everything I had is here for the cost of a decent dinner, and you do not need a diagnosis, a referral, or anyone's permission to start.
